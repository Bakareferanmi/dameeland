About dameen

dameen is a student community where people learn, build, play and connect. Students can collaborate with friends, share posts, explore lessons, take on games and challenges, and turn ideas into things worth sharing.

Open dameen

What can students study?

Learn includes mathematics, English and digital technologies. Lessons explain topics such as factorising quadratics, subject–verb agreement and binary place value with worked examples and practice questions.

How does daily practice work?

A different lesson is featured each day. Students can also choose a lesson by subject, use the study timer to focus, and sign in to save completed lessons.

Do I need an account?

Create an account to save learning progress, view shared posts and profiles, and message classmates.
